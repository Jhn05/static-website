My Old Art

00:00:00 Main Page Author Profile Interests Highlights

Drawing; Like I have written in the interests page, I had studied various art styles from various mangakas while reading manga, I was active in doing so during the pandemic. I don't have any memorable events in my life because I think that everything is insignificant in the grand scheme of things.

So here are few of the many drawings that I've produced during that time;


'Him'

Digitally drawn with a brush setting that's close to a pen, and a canvas that mimics the appearance of paper



'Astral'

Lazily and digitally painted, unfinished, unsatisfied. Usually when I'm not sastisfied with my art I immediately trash it. But for some reason I didn't trash this one.



'Fio'

A sketch of Fiolina Germi, a character from the game called Metal Slug. I forgot why I drew a sketch of her. Maybe it was a trend, or maybe I was playing Metal Slug at that time. Regardless, I liked how the sketch turned out.



'Top Hat Forg'

Tried practicing animals, got lazy to do anatomically accurate ones so I did stylized drawings. This one turned out great but he was boring, so I gave him a top hat.It's just digital painting of a distinguished frog, but ain't he a gentleman!
